About

From biology to broadcast.

I graduated with a B.Sc. (Hons) in Biology from Delhi University — where I learned to observe, research and spot patterns. Today I apply that same curiosity to media: finding the content and creators that make a streaming platform worth watching.

At SWIFT TV (The Germane Media) I source and acquire content for FAST channels, run market research and facilitate partnerships with creators and media companies. Before that I supported go‑to‑market work at Escape Tribe with research, video editing and social advertising.

"Great content isn't found by chance — it's found by listening to audiences and building trust with the people who create it."

I'm Microsoft Office Specialist and McKinsey.org Forward certified, and I lean on AI tools to work smarter, faster and more creatively.
